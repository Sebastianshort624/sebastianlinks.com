Based on the information you provided, we identified several significant circumstances surrounding your original purchase, subsequent upgrade, and continuing ownership experience. These circumstances form the basis for Client Protection Group LLC accepting your case for cancellation services.

Client's Reported Findings

You attended the resort for a tour and believed you were purchasing a timeshare at the resort or location where you were taking the tour. You later discovered that the ownership was actually associated with Branson — a fact you did not learn until approximately one year later, when you attended an owners update.

At that time, you were told that the salesperson who originally sold you the timeshare no longer worked there because of what happened. You were subsequently told that the only way to obtain the location you actually wanted was to upgrade your contract. The upgrade resulted in a larger monthly and annual maintenance obligation.

You reported that you were never told the total price of the timeshare before signing, and that you did not know the total price until after you had already signed the contract. You report that you still cannot get the availability you expected and cannot reliably book what you wanted.

You stopped making payments — not because you could not afford them, but because of your dissatisfaction with the circumstances surrounding the ownership. You were later asked to attend an owners update that would require flying to the location and sitting through several hours of an owners presentation, and you were told you could be charged if you did not attend. You described this as the final straw that caused you to stop making payments.

You want out of the ownership, and you reported feeling completely lied to and scammed.

Consumer Rights Review

The following presents a professional consumer-rights assessment based solely on the facts you reported. This section does not constitute a legal determination, and no government agency has evaluated or ruled on these circumstances.

Right I — The Right to Safety

You reported being told you could be charged if you did not attend a required owners update involving travel and several hours of presentation — an ongoing pressure you described as the final straw before you stopped making payments.

Right II — The Right to Choose

You reported being told that an upgrade was the only way to obtain the location you wanted, and that you were not told the total price of the timeshare before signing — circumstances you say affected your ability to make a fully informed purchasing decision.

Right III — The Right to Be Heard

You reported that your dissatisfaction with the ownership experience — including the location discrepancy and the upgrade — was not resolved, and that this ultimately led you to stop making payments.

Right IV — The Right to Be Informed

You reported discovering only about a year later that the ownership was associated with Branson rather than the location of the tour, and that you did not learn the total purchase price until after signing.
