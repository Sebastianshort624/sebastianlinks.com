🎯 90-Minute Appointment

4 stages · 45 + 15 + 15 + 15
Client:

STAGE 1 — GET TO YES 45 MIN

0:00–0:45 · intro → credibility → listen → CRM → violations → price → YES
  1. "My role is to make sure the case we build is strong enough to bring to our approval team. The reason being we have a 99% success rate because we only take on cases we know we will win — because we give a 100% money-back guarantee, and it costs us money as we progress your case."
  2. "Did they show you we are pioneers of the cancellation industry, the industry we created?"
  3. "What exactly were you told that convinced you to purchase?"
  4. "If the stars align and my assessment team decides we can take on your case, is there anything at all that would prevent you from moving forward with us today?"
  5. "We will never take on your case unless we can save you a ridiculous amount of money."
→ Price accepted. Move to payment.

STAGE 2 — FIND THE MONEY 15 MIN

0:45–1:00 · checking/savings → short exactly → credit cards
  1. "The fastest, easiest, and smartest way to do so financially is: if you can pay it out of any checking or savings account. If you have the money, then well, pay it."
  2. "Do you have it in your checking account, savings account, or any type of retirement account, 401(k), IRA, anything like that, stocks, bonds, whatever, where you could get it into your checking account within a certain amount of days?"
  3. "About how far off are you from that number, exactly?"
  4. "So what I'm hearing from you is that you have $________ that you could put towards this. Is that what I'm hearing from you?"
  5. "Okay, so the second way most people pay for our services is with credit cards."
→ Financing? Go to Stage 3. Cash/card? Collect DL + bank proof, skip to Stage 4.

STAGE 3 — APPLY FOR CREDIT 15 MIN

1:00–1:15 · qualify → application info
  1. "The third option is financing it and breaking it into monthly payments."
  2. "Do you have any idea what your credit score is?"
  3. "You said that you can put down $________. So then we'd be looking at breaking the remaining into monthly payments."
  4. "Let's go ahead and see what we can get you qualified for."
  5. "Now, a couple things I'm going to need is can you guys send me pictures of your driver's licenses?"
→ Place them on hold — case goes to the approval team.

STAGE 4 — SIGN DOCUMENTS 15 MIN

1:15–1:30 · post-hold → prep agreement → DocuSign
  1. "Congratulations one of our case managers has decided to take your case. The total price is $________ — that includes the entire abolition of your entire contractual obligation — we will never ask you for another penny."
  2. "So what I'll need in order to move forward is: what I'm going to do next. I'm going to work on preparing our service agreement, right?"
  3. "It's going to take me about 10 minutes to prepare."
  4. "Do you know how to send pictures from your phone?"
  5. "We will never ask you for another penny."
