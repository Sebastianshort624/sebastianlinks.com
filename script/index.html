STAGE 2 — HOW WE WIN 15 MIN

0:15–0:30 · let them talk (5 min) → violations & how we win (10 min)
🔗 sebastianlinks.com/calc — cost of ownership calculator
  1. "Now that you've seen who we are and what our reviews say — I want to hear your story. Tell me about your experience with your timeshare." — let them talk
  2. "What exactly were you told that convinced you to purchase?" — the main five, in plain talk:
    1. When you bought, did they make it sound like you could always just sell it if you wanted out? These things are listed on eBay for a dollar — you can't even give them away.
    2. Did they tell you the maintenance fees never end — that they go on forever and get passed down to your kids?
    3. What was the maintenance fee back when you bought? And did they tell you it goes up every single year — around 8%?
    4. Did they make it sound like you had to be an owner to stay in these resorts — priority benefits, good availability, whatever you wanted whenever? [CA name] showed you that's not true — you can rent the same rooms anytime for a fraction of the cost. Remember seeing rooms for like a hundred bucks a night? A whole week for 700 bucks — while as an owner you're paying thousands a year and you're lucky to get a single week.
    5. And did they tell you booking was easy — just call a few days or weeks ahead? What was it actually like when you tried?
  3. "We take all of this to the resort and lay out the fines they're facing — so they see it's cheaper to let you out than to risk paying those fines."
  4. "And we go after compensation for the emotional and financial impact this had on you. Our competitors settle cases by getting the client to pay the resort — we do the opposite, and our reviews prove it."
  5. "We will never take on your case unless we can save you a ridiculous amount of money."
→ Violations documented. Now the approval process.
