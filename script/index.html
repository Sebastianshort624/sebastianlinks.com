⚖️ LINX LEGAL — ESSENTIAL VIOLATION-DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

1. SALES PRESENTATION & PRESSURE

  • How long did the sales presentation last?
  • Were you told it would only take 90 minutes?
  • How many different salespeople did you speak with?
  • How many times did you say NO?
  • Did you ask to leave or end the presentation? What happened?
  • Did you feel exhausted, confused, overwhelmed, or pressured?
  • Did they separate you from your spouse or focus on one of you?
  • Were you told the offer or price was only available that day?
  • Were you given gifts, meals, drinks, or other incentives?
Notes:

2. PROMISES & REPRESENTATIONS

  • What exactly were you told that convinced you to purchase?
  • Were you told you could rent your timeshare for income?
  • Were you told you could sell it or recover your purchase price?
  • Was it presented as an investment or something that would increase in value?
  • Were you promised availability, exchanges, or benefits you couldn't actually use?
  • Were you promised refinancing, a buyback, or help selling or renting?
Notes:

3. FINANCIAL DISCLOSURES

  • Were the maintenance fees fully explained before signing?
  • Were you told maintenance fees could increase every year?
  • Were you told maintenance fees could continue indefinitely?
  • Were you told the financial obligations could affect your children or estate?
  • Were the interest rate, total interest, and total purchase cost explained?
  • Were you shown the true cost over 5, 10, or 20 years?
  • Were you asked to open credit cards or obtain additional financing?
Notes:

4. CONTRACT & CANCELLATION

  • Were you given enough time to read and understand the entire contract?
  • Was the contract in a language you understood?
  • Did you discover terms afterward that were not explained?
  • Were you told you could cancel the timeshare?
  • Was the rescission or cancellation period explained?
  • Did you try to cancel? What happened?
  • Were you told what could happen if you stopped paying?
Notes:

5. UPGRADES & ADDITIONAL PURCHASES

  • Were you told an upgrade would solve an existing problem?
  • Were you promised lower fees or better availability?
  • Did the upgrade actually deliver what was promised?
  • Were you told you had to purchase more points or another contract?
Notes:

6. DECISION IMPACT — MOST IMPORTANT

  • If you had known then what you know today, would you still have purchased?
  • What specific promise or information influenced your decision to sign?
  • What did you later discover was different from what you were told?
  • Which of those facts would have changed your decision?
  • How has that affected you financially and emotionally?
