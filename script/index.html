🎯 90-Minute Appointment

6 stages · 15 min each
Client:

STAGE 1 — INTRODUCTION & CREDIBILITY 15 MIN

0:00–0:15 · chit-chat (5 min) → credibility research together (10 min)
  1. "My role is to make sure the case we build is strong enough to bring to our approval team. The reason being we have a 99% success rate because we only take on cases we know we will win — because we give a 100% money-back guarantee, and it costs us money as we progress your case."
  2. "I see you and [CA name] researched our company, so are you sure you have no questions re Linx Legal the company and our ability to do what we say we will do?"
  3. "Did they show you we are pioneers of the cancellation industry, the industry we created?"
  4. Research together: sebastianlinks.com/linx — company credibility & reviews.
  5. "You saw our reviews yourself — that is our reputation."
→ Credibility established. Now let them talk.

STAGE 2 — HOW WE WIN 15 MIN

0:15–0:30 · let them talk (5 min) → violations & how we win (10 min)
  1. "Now that you've seen who we are and what our reviews say — I want to hear your story. Tell me about your experience with your timeshare." — let them talk
  2. "What exactly were you told that convinced you to purchase?" — the main five violation questions:
    1. Did they tell you you could sell it? (False sense of security — timeshares listed on eBay for $1.)
    2. Did they disclose the perpetuity clause — that maintenance fees go on forever?
    3. What was the maintenance fee at point of purchase? Did they mention fees increasing ~8%?
    4. Right-to-choose: they promised "priority privilege" — yet RedWeek has lower prices, better availability, no owner presentations.
    5. Right-to-choose: they told you reserving dates was a matter of days or weeks in advance — what was it really? Limited availability.
  3. "We take these violations and present the fines to the resort, so they see it's cheaper to release you than to risk paying those fines."
  4. "And we go after compensation for the emotional and financial impact this had on you. Our competitors settle cases by getting the client to pay the resort — we do the opposite, and our reviews prove it."
  5. "We will never take on your case unless we can save you a ridiculous amount of money."
→ Violations documented. Now the approval process.

STAGE 3 — THE APPROVAL PROCESS 15 MIN

0:30–0:45 · selectivity & approval (5 min) → scarcity & waiting list (5 min) → the YES (5 min)
  1. "We win 99% of our cases by being very selective — we only take on cases we know we will win."
  2. "Approval is a combination of two things: the strength of your case, and whether a case manager is willing to take it on."
  3. "A case manager takes your case based on their opinion of the probability of getting the resort to pay compensation — that is what drives their availability."
  4. "If no case manager can take your case right now, we do have a waiting list."
  5. "If the stars align and my assessment team decides we can take on your case, is there anything at all that would prevent you from moving forward with us today?"
→ YES secured. Move to payment.

STAGE 4 — FIND THE MONEY 15 MIN

0:45–1:00 · checking/savings (5 min) → how far off exactly (5 min) → credit cards (5 min)
  1. "The fastest, easiest, and smartest way to do so financially is: if you can pay it out of any checking or savings account. If you have the money, then well, pay it."
  2. "Do you have it in your checking account, savings account, or any type of retirement account, 401(k), IRA, anything like that, stocks, bonds, whatever, where you could get it into your checking account within a certain amount of days?"
  3. "About how far off are you from that number, exactly?"
  4. "So what I'm hearing from you is that you have $________ that you could put towards this. Is that what I'm hearing from you?"
  5. "Okay, so the second way most people pay for our services is with credit cards."
→ Financing? Apply now. Cash/card? Collect DL + bank proof, skip to Stage 6.

STAGE 5 — APPLY FOR CREDIT 15 MIN

1:00–1:15 · monthly payments (5 min) → credit score & down payment (5 min) → application info (5 min)
  1. "The third option is financing it and breaking it into monthly payments."
  2. "Do you have any idea what your credit score is?"
  3. "You said that you can put down $________. So then we'd be looking at breaking the remaining into monthly payments."
  4. "Let's go ahead and see what we can get you qualified for."
  5. "Now, a couple things I'm going to need is can you guys send me pictures of your driver's licenses?"
→ Place them on hold — case goes to the approval team.

STAGE 6 — SIGN DOCUMENTS 15 MIN

1:15–1:30 · post-hold good news (5 min) → prepare the agreement (10 min)
  1. "Good news — there are two potential case managers that can take this case tomorrow. One of them, she's very experienced at ___. The other one, he's amazing at ___."
  2. "Congratulations one of our case managers has decided to take your case. The total price is $________ — that includes the entire abolition of your entire contractual obligation — we will never ask you for another penny."
  3. "So what I'll need in order to move forward is: what I'm going to do next. I'm going to work on preparing our service agreement, right?"
  4. "It's going to take me about 10 minutes to prepare."
  5. "Do you know how to send pictures from your phone?"
