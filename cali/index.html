INCIVA Natural History Museum

Museo Departamental de Ciencias Naturales Federico Carlos Lehmann
Hours Mon–Fri 8am–4pm · Sat 10am–3pm · Sun 10am–2pm · Guided tours hourly
Admission Adults $10,000 COP · Students $7,500 COP · Seniors/disability $5,000 · Foreign visitors 10 USD
Address Av. Roosevelt (Calle 6 #24-80), Piso 4, Cali
Phone +(57) 2 514 68 48

Founded in 1963 by biologist-ornithologist Federico Carlos Lehmann Valencia, this departmental museum celebrates the stunning biodiversity of Valle del Cauca — from cloud-forest orchids to Andean condors. Guided tours depart every hour.

inciva.gov.co Google Maps

Planetario de Cali — YAWA

Centro de Ciencia, Arte y Tecnología
Hours 9:00am – 6:00pm daily · Shows at 9am, 11am, 1pm, 3pm, 5pm
Admission ~$17,000–20,000 COP · Ages 4+ · Buy at TuBoleta.com Reserve ahead
Address Cra. 37a #5-27, San Fernando (frente al Hospital Universitario del Valle), Cali

Opened December 2024 — Cali's first planetarium and the second largest in Colombia. A fulldome theater with immersive shows on astronomy and biodiversity. Part of YAWA, a 23,000 m² center for science, art, and technology.

yawacali.com Book tickets Maps

Museo del Oro Calima

Banco de la República · Centro Histórico
Hours Tue–Fri 9am–4pm · Sat 10am–4pm · Sun/Mon closed
Admission FREE — no ticket required Entrada libre
Address Calle 7 #4-69, Centro Histórico, Cali
Coords 3.4500°N, 76.5357°W

615 pre-Columbian masterworks in gold, ceramics, stone, and bone — from the Ilama, Yotoco, and Sonso peoples who shaped this valley for millennia. One of six Banco de la República gold museums in Colombia. Free admission, no exceptions.

banrepcultural.org Maps

Museo Arqueológico La Merced

Est. 1979 · Colonial Convent · Oldest Complex in Cali
Hours Mon–Sat · Call ahead to confirm: (602) 885-5309
Admission ~$4,000 COP · Cash only · No cards accepted
Address Cra. 4 #6-59, San Pedro, Cali
Email museolamerced@une.net.co

Six permanent rooms of pre-Columbian ceramics, funerary urns, and artifacts from the Calima, Tumaco, Nariño, Quimbaya, and Tierradentro cultures — housed inside a 17th-century convent attached to La Merced Church, the oldest standing structure in Cali. The interior courtyard alone is worth the visit.
