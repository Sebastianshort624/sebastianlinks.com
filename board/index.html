How to read this board (v3): the top sections come from the SOLD RATIO tab — its status and its pending note (under Obstacles) govern for those clients; below are the /script records that are not on the tab. Conflicts between sources are flagged in red on the row, never resolved silently. Direct editing: tap the ★ to rate, change the status with the dropdown (the row moves sections), click Obstacles to write your note, adjust the FU Date — and set a time next to it to make it an FU ST (the timed date also creates the Pipeline FU task) — and log each contact with LVM / TEXT / CALL — everything saves to the cloud instantly and no sync wipes your edits. Click a lead's Total to change its price: type a new total, or type a discount like 10% to take that percent off the current total — the new price saves to the board and pushes straight into that client's /script record (Pipeline follows within ~10 minutes). The Payoff column shows the balance on file and 47% the standard mortgage price off it (deed-backs never price off a % — they show —). The 💲 CAT 1–9 button next to the price chips opens the category legend. Each lead's Date cell carries a cohort badge — NEW (green: this appointment is the lead's first on file), RS · from … (amber: an earlier appointment exists, dated), DB (orange: deed-back). The 🌙 NIGHT AUDIT button tallies today the LAP way — CB = today's NEW + RS leads, deed-backs excluded, outcomes of that set only — with a copy button for the nightly post. The 💳 48-MO PLAN button lists follow-ups whose notes show a UCFS denial or bad credit, each with a ready-to-send in-house proposal (30% down split over 6 monthly payments, then 48 monthly payments, straight split, no interest), plus a best-guess stall reason for every follow-up. After you change a price, the change is logged under the Total (↓ % of payoff on a mortgage, ↓ pad on a deed-back — never the $ amount discounted) with 📱 TEXTED / ✉️ EMAILED marks and a 🕘 history — bookkeeping only, the board never sends anything. The FU Date sits next to the phones: if it says ✓ TASK, that date came from your FU task in Pipeline (it beats any event); if it says event, it came from a calendar event. Search criteria (top): tap chips to combine — once = YES, twice = NO, third tap = off: YES TODAY + YES DEED-BACK shows today's deed-backs; TODAY also pulls in every lead whose Pipeline task is due today or overdue (per CRM). The two date boxes filter by ANY date on the lead — appointment, FU date, close date or task due date. Each lead's next Pipeline task shows under its FU date; its ✓ MARK COMPLETE button marks it done here and queues it to be completed in Pipeline; YES SOLD + NO DEED-BACK shows mortgage solds only. The search box also filters every section by name, resort, phone or status. Every date is captioned lead by lead: CLOSE DATE on sold leads (plus CHARGE DATE and PAID DATE on charged deals), FU DATE on follow-ups, RESCHEDULED TO on reschedules. Hide and sort: each section has its own button to hide or show it and its own menu to sort the rows — by name, appointment date, FU date, sale date, pay day, deal size (total), commission, stars, payoff, category or status; dates run soonest-first, money and stars biggest-first, and rows missing that key sink to the bottom — your choice saves in this browser and survives syncs. Per-lead dropdown: under each lead's status there is a small dropdown that states its facts at a glance — on sold deals whether it is CHARGED, whether the commission is PAID (with the date) or PENDING, and the pay day; on follow-ups the FU date (and the set time on an FU ST) and the appointment on file; on no-answers the appointment that no-answered.
