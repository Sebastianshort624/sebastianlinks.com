How to read this board (v3): the top sections come from the SOLD RATIO tab — its status and its pending note (under Obstacles) govern for those clients; below are the /script records that are not on the tab. Conflicts between sources are flagged in red on the row, never resolved silently. Direct editing: tap the ★ to rate, change the status with the dropdown (the row moves sections), click Obstacles to write your note, adjust the FU Date and log each contact with LVM / TEXT / CALL — everything saves to the cloud instantly and no sync wipes your edits. The FU Date sits next to the phones: if it says ✓ TASK, that date came from your FU task in Pipeline (it beats any event); if it says event, it came from a calendar event. Search criteria (top): tap chips to combine — once = YES, twice = NO, third tap = off: YES TODAY + YES DEED-BACK shows today's deed-backs; YES SOLD + NO DEED-BACK shows mortgage solds only. The search box also filters every section by name, resort, phone or status. Every date is captioned lead by lead: CLOSE DATE on sold leads (plus CHARGE DATE and PAID DATE on charged deals), FU DATE on follow-ups, RESCHEDULED TO on reschedules. Hide and sort: each section has its own button to hide or show it and its own menu to sort the rows (name, commission, total, signed date, payday or status) — your choice saves in this browser and survives syncs. CHARGED in two sections: if the commission payday has arrived (today or earlier), the row lands in COMMISSION PAID; if the payday is in the future or there is no payday, it stays in COMMISSION UNPAID — and it moves to paid on its own when the payday arrives. To force a row into the paid section without waiting for the payday, set its status to CHARGED-PAID in the row's status dropdown. Mortgage commission: CC/ACH 10% · UCFS 8% · UGA 4% on (total − fees − $299; fees = 3% on CC, $199 on the rest; UGA only deducts the $199). Deed-back commission: $300 + ½ of the pad; pad = total − list price − $299 − card fee if applicable. Deed-backs live separately in their 🏠 DEED-BACK sections (they do not count against the closing ratio); if the list price is not on file, the row shows "—" under List Price and keeps its recorded commission, and payroll-verified commissions (P8C) beat the formula. If there is no confirmed payment type or total, the commission shows "—" and the reason goes under Obstacles.
